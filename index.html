01 — About

The job is the system, not the screenshot.

Four years as a DevOps engineer on an e-commerce company whose site and web app process thousands of transactions every day. The work was CI/CD, infrastructure, performance, monitoring, and keeping releases from becoming outages.

Alongside that, freelance full stack development for automation industries: different plants, different processes, one requirement — software that operators can actually run.

  • Ship through a pipeline, not a laptop.
  • Measure the path a transaction takes.
  • Industrial software has to match the process, not the other way around.
  • Reliability is a feature the customer never sees until it fails.
